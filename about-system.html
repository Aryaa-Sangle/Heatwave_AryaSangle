About the Heatwave Intelligence System

Objective

The system aims to monitor, predict and issue early warnings for heatwave conditions across regions.

Data Sources

Data is collected from AWS sensors, the IMD database, and satellite feeds.

Stakeholders

Key stakeholders include meteorologists, disaster-management officers, and the general public.

"Timely heatwave alerts save lives. This system is our first line of defence against extreme heat." - Chief Meteorologist
